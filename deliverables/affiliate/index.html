PARTNER PROGRAM / FAQ
Is any commission rate live or guaranteed?+

No. Neither the 25% example nor the 5–30% calculator range is guaranteed. Quadcode must approve the actual rate structure, eligible plans and partner agreement before any public launch.

How often do partners get paid?+

Payout cadence, currency, payment rails, minimum threshold and tax requirements need confirmation. This preview intentionally shows no fabricated payout claim.

How is a referral tracked?+

A production program needs unique links, a documented attribution window, consent-compliant tracking and a dashboard that distinguishes clicks, eligible sales, refunds and settled commissions.

Can I share conceptual content or use discounts?+

Show your own genuine results and clearly disclose any partnership. Examples on this page are illustrative art direction, not verified customer work. Never advertise an audience discount or specific commission payout (rates in this model span 5–30% purely as examples) until approved and live.

Does the form submit a real application?+

Not yet. The brief builder is a demo: it prepares a shareable request locally but does not transmit your details. The team must connect a secure intake endpoint before launch.
