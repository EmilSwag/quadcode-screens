02–04 · Website & sign-up

quadcode.ai: give a reason to sign up now

Today the site has no offer, no timer, no sign-up bonus and no pricing link. Higgsfield repeats its offer in the header, in banners and inside the sign-up flow. We add one strip, one nav link and a one-screen auth modal that mirrors theirs — creative story on the left, equal-size providers on the right.

Starter Kit store, exit offer, top-up packs with a fixed loader, a plan card that shows value, fair proration, a one-screen cancel and referrals. One urgency offer at a time; timers are server deadlines that really end.

17–24 · Profile, bonus & workspace

One account, one balance — the same on the website and in the IDE

A profile menu with a live balance, a one-click “Claim your bonus” with coins and a count-up, guest passes, and a workspace that finally shows credits, results and forecast. Use the Demo account switcher at the bottom of the screen: every synced block (15, 17, 18, 19) changes together. Press “Claim 200 QCC” anywhere — the website and the IDE update at once.

03 Offer rules (meta/rules/temporary-offers.md)

  • ✓ One urgency offer at a time. Exit (+30%) replaces Starter Kit (+20%) for 5 minutes, never stacks.
  • ✓ Deadlines are server timestamps. Reload, new device, IDE — same second.
  • ✓ Expired means expired. No auto-restart, no "extended for you".
  • ✓ Bonus credits instead of fake anchor prices. The only struck price is the real yearly list price ($240 → $180).
  • ✓ Decline buttons are neutral ("No thanks, keep browsing"). Cancel is one screen, always reachable.
  • ✗ No countdown without a real deadline object. No dark-pattern confirm chains.
